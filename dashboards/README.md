Save your completed `Call_Center_Analytics.pbix` here after validation. No PBIX is included in this starter repository.

Suggested pages: Executive Overview; Channel Performance; Customer Experience; Operational Performance; Geographic Analysis.

Use `analytics.call_center` for interactive measures and use SQL summary views for cross-checks. Validate unfiltered totals against `outputs/tables/overall_kpis.csv`.

Before sharing screenshots, ensure they do not display customer names or individual-level identifiers.
